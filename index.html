THINK BEYOND WHAT YOU KNOW

Every story deserves context.

A story can be meaningful while still representing one perspective. Explore thoughtfully and make room for different voices.

01

Notice your first impression

Ask yourself what you already believe about a community or tradition before reading. Be willing to question assumptions.

02

Look for another perspective

Compare accounts from different contributors and generations. Avoid treating one story as the experience of an entire community.

03

Check the source and context

Look for the storyteller, date, language and community review. If these details are missing, treat the content as unverified.

Our promise: Hadithi Zetu aims to reduce confirmation bias by inviting respectful questions, multiple perspectives and source checking—not by telling users what to believe.
